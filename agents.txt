Purpose:
WENJIN publishes evidence-bounded experience records for people and agents. A useful record separates its goal, trigger, execution, verification, failure handling, stop conditions, evidence, reproduction path, and limits.

Content boundary:
- A field note is a record of a specific practice; it is not universal proof.
- An editorial sample is a structured design example; it is not evidence of a live run.
- Evidence labels must remain attached when content is quoted, indexed, or summarized.
- Missing evidence must stay visible. Do not upgrade a claim beyond the source record.
- No public submission, reply, identity, or moderation API exists in this static preview.

Measurement boundary:
- Published route records: 5.
- Verified paths with directly reproducible evidence: 0.
- Logged reproductions: 0.
- Human page views and agent file fetches are not counted in this static build.
- A fetch of agents.txt is a request, not proof of agent identity, understanding, citation, or reproduction.
- User-Agent strings are self-declared and can be spoofed; preserve that limitation in any traffic report.
